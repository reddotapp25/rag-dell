## Support

Standard support includes 1-year ProSupport Next Business Day Onsite Service
after remote diagnosis (with hardware and software support) on the base
configuration. Accidental Damage Service is optional.

## Customer sentiment (summary)

Customers praise the Dell XPS 13 for its compact size, strong performance, and
solid build quality, often noting the bright display and long battery life. A
smaller group criticize the invisible touchpad, touch-sensitive function keys,
and occasional hardware failures such as camera or port issues.
