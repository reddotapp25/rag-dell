# Laptop Battery Not Charging: Resolve AC Adapter Issues

Source: https://www.dell.com/support/contents/en-us/article/product-support/self-support-knowledgebase/battery-and-power/ac-adapter
Company: Dell

Like most laptops, Dell laptops use Lithium-ion batteries, which can decline
in performance over time due to age, charge cycles, or exposure to extreme
temperatures. If your laptop suddenly stops charging or the battery won't
hold a charge, this article helps you diagnose and resolve charging issues.

## Common causes of battery charging issues

- Faulty AC adapter: The charger might be malfunctioning, not supplying
  adequate power, or not compatible with your laptop.
- Battery hardware issues: The battery itself could be faulty, damaged, or
  experiencing failure in its internal circuitry, or simply aging.
- Charging-port problems: Dust, debris, or a damaged charging port can
  prevent the laptop from charging.
- Software and driver issues: Outdated BIOS, drivers, or software can
  affect charging.
- Power settings: Incorrect power configurations can reduce charging
  efficiency.

## How to resolve battery charging problems

### Checking the power connection
1. Ensure the power outlet works by plugging in another device.
2. Try plugging the charger into a different outlet to rule out the outlet.

### Inspecting the charger, charging port, and cable
1. Inspect the charging port for debris, dust, or lint. Use compressed air
   to blow out any debris gently.
2. Inspect the entire charging cable for fraying or bent pins. Replace the
   cable or charger if there are signs of wear or damage.

### Performing a hard reset
1. Turn off the laptop.
2. Disconnect the power adapter and remove the battery (if applicable).
3. Disconnect all peripherals, including external monitors and docking
   stations.
4. Press and hold the power button for 15–20 seconds.
5. Reconnect the battery (if applicable) and the power adapter, then turn
   on the laptop.

### Reinstalling battery drivers
A corrupted driver in Windows can cause charging issues.

1. Right-click Start and select Device Manager.
2. Expand Batteries.
3. Right-click "Microsoft ACPI-Compliant Control Method Battery" and
   select Uninstall device.
4. Restart your computer. Windows automatically reinstalls the necessary
   drivers.

### Updating BIOS and drivers
Outdated BIOS and drivers are a common cause. SupportAssist is Dell's
automated tool that scans your Windows computer for driver and firmware
updates, then downloads and installs them.

1. Open SupportAssist from the Start menu.
2. On the Home tab, select Update Software.
3. Click Start to check for driver and BIOS updates.
4. Follow the on-screen instructions and restart if prompted.

### Running a Dell battery test
1. Go to the SupportAssist battery test (dell.com/support/diagnose/home/
   index/battery). Install SupportAssist if prompted.
2. Follow the on-screen instructions and respond to prompts.
3. The diagnostic results appear after the test.
4. If a hardware issue is detected, you may be guided to update drivers or
   contact Dell Technical Support for repair options.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why is my Dell laptop plugged in but not charging?**
This could be due to a faulty charger, battery issues, or software problems.
Follow the steps in this guide.

**How do I check my battery health?**
Use the Windows battery report, Dell apps such as Dell Power Manager or
Dell Optimizer, or a SupportAssist diagnostic test.

**Is it safe to use third-party chargers?**
It is recommended to use only genuine Dell chargers to avoid compatibility
and safety issues.

**What does "plugged in, not charging" mean?**
The battery isn't accepting a charge. It could be due to an incompatible
charger, a corrupted battery driver, or hardware issues with the battery.

**What should I do if my laptop won't turn on at all?**
Check the charger connections; use the original charger; perform a hard
reset; turn off the laptop, leave the charger plugged in, and let the
battery charge.

**Can running too many applications affect charging?**
Yes, high resource usage can impact performance and charging efficiency.

**How do I know if my charger is working properly?**
- Use the original charger.
- Make sure the LED on the charger stays on when plugged in. If it turns
  off, contact Dell Technical Support for repair options.
- Check for damage to the charger, cables, or pins.
- Check the AC adapter status in BIOS settings; the wattage should be
  correctly recognized. "Unknown" means the charger is incompatible or
  malfunctioning.
- Test the charger with another compatible laptop if possible.

**Can I use my Dell laptop without a battery?**
Yes, but the laptop will turn off immediately during a power failure.

**What should I do if my laptop battery charges slowly?**
Use the original charger, check for driver and BIOS updates, and inspect
the charging port for debris.

**Will a BIOS/UEFI update fix my battery charging issue?**
A BIOS/UEFI update can resolve certain software-related charging issues.
Ensure your BIOS/UEFI is up to date.

**Why does my laptop charger get hot?**
Chargers generate some heat, but overheating can occur due to poor
ventilation, incompatible chargers, or internal issues. Ensure good airflow
around the charger.

**Why is my laptop charger making a buzzing sound?**
A buzzing sound may indicate electrical issues. Discontinue use immediately
and contact Dell Technical Support.

**Can I use my laptop charger internationally?**
Yes, modern Dell chargers support a wide range of input voltages (typically
100–240 V) and frequencies (50–60 Hz). Use the appropriate plug adapter.

**What are the best practices for using laptop chargers?**
- Use Dell-approved chargers; avoid third-party chargers to prevent
  compatibility and safety issues.
- Plug the adapter directly into a wall outlet instead of power strips.
- Disconnect the charger from both the laptop and the outlet when not
  charging.
- Avoid bending or twisting cables; store the charger in a cool, dry
  place.
- Periodically check for damage or wear on the adapter and cables.

**Where can I find the specs of my laptop charger?**
On the sticker on the back of the charger: wattage, input voltage, output
voltage, and model number.

**How do I charge my laptop using a docking station?**
Use the charger that came with your docking station, ensure the docking
station is compatible with your laptop, and connect to the correct port
on your laptop (not all USB-C ports support power delivery).

**What is an Auto/Air adapter?**
A versatile power adapter that charges a laptop from a car's cigarette-
lighter socket or an airplane power outlet. Compatible with select
Latitude, Vostro, and Precision laptops.

**What if none of these steps work?**
Try additional battery-charging troubleshooting (KB 000123069) and visit
the Dell Community Forums.
