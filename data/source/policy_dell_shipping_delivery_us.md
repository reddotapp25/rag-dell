# Dell Shipping and Delivery (US)

Source: https://www.dell.com/en-us/lp/dell-free-shipping
Company: Dell

## Free shipping

- Everyone gets free standard delivery on everything, with no minimum
  purchase.
- Dell Rewards members get free expedited delivery on eligible products.

## 2-Day Delivery (Sunday 2-Day Delivery)

Available in select markets for certain products sold on Dell.com.
- Order before 2:00 PM Central Time (Monday through Saturday, excluding
  holidays) and select Expedited Delivery at checkout.
- Where available, 2-Day Delivery orders received Monday through Friday by
  the 2:00 PM CT cutoff ship the same day for delivery within 2 days after
  shipment (excluding holidays).
- For specific availability, click "View Delivery Dates" on the applicable
  product page and enter your shipping postal code.

## 2-Day Delivery conditions and limits

- Available only for certain products in select zip codes in the contiguous
  United States. Excluded: Alaska, Hawaii, US Territories, and APO/FPO
  addresses.
- Your order must be placed, and payment successfully charged, before the
  cutoff time.
- Delivery dates may become unavailable due to changes in inventory or
  delivery capacity before you place your order. The confirmed delivery
  date is included in the order-confirmation email.
- Order-size limitations may apply.
- Any changes made to an order after it is placed may impact delivery
  timing.
- Electronics, accessories, and other non-eligible items in the same order
  may ship separately and have a different delivery date.
- Orders financed through Dell Financial Services may be subject to a
  verification hold that affects delivery date.
- 2-Day Delivery may be impacted by unforeseen circumstances beyond Dell's
  control: strikes, natural disasters, pandemics, severe weather, holidays,
  or busy times such as Black Friday and Cyber Monday.

## Expedited and Express Delivery conditions

- Applies only to PCs, Electronics, and Accessories that ship within 24
  hours.
- Limited quantities available.
- Expedited and express delivery are not available for monitors that are
  55 inches or larger.
- Credit card approved or DPA orders only.
- Not available for APO/FPO, Alaska, or Hawaii orders.
- Electronics and accessories may ship separately.
- Dell is not responsible for carrier errors or delays.

## Shipping methods and carriers

Shipping methods available:
- Express Shipping
- Expedited Shipping
- Standard Ground Shipping

Exclusions apply (certain TVs, monitors, and batteries). Dell partners with
leading carriers and selects the most appropriate carrier for each shipment.
Tracking numbers and links are visible from My Account → Orders →
Track Order. The shipping method chosen at purchase is applied after the
order has completed production/packaging, not during assembly.

## Build-to-order vs. in-stock

- In-Stock products typically have an estimated delivery time of 2–4
  business days from order confirmation for shipment.
- If an order contains both a Dell system and accessories, these may be
  delivered separately.
- For Build-to-Order products, Dell configures to your specifications and
  provides a "ships in" date range; delivery happens after that shipment.

## Order splitting and lead times

- Depending on products ordered and their production times or availability,
  an order may be split into multiple order numbers with different
  estimated delivery dates.
- Orders shipped from overseas locations may have extended lead times:
  roughly 7–10 business days for USA, 9–14 business days for Canada.
- Orders placed on or before 02:00 PM CST are eligible for
  expedited/express shipping.
- Orders shipped after business hours are considered to have shipped on the
  next business day.
- APO/DPO/FPO ship-to addresses are shipped from the U.S. and can take up
  to 8 weeks to arrive; these shipments cannot be tracked during transit.
- Sometimes carriers reuse a tracking number; if you see an older delivery
  date on a newly-provided tracking number, wait 24 hours for updates.
