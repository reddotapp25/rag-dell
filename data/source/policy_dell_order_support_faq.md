# Dell Order Support FAQ

Source: https://www.dell.com/support/contents/en-us/article/esupport-order-support/frequently-asked-questions
Company: Dell

A curated Dell FAQ covering order status, payment, shipping, delivery,
cancellations, returns, taxes, and financing for US consumers.

## Order status

Typical statuses:

- Order Processing / Processing: Dell has received the order but payment
  has not yet cleared. Sign in to My Account and select "Orders" to view
  status.
- Payment Issue: There may have been a problem processing your card.
  Check email for a secure re-processing link, or in My Account click
  "Retry Payment".
- In Production (Dell-branded products): Once payment authorization is
  received, Dell-branded products move to production.
- Getting Ready for Packaging (non-Dell-branded products): Items sourced
  from partners move to packaging rather than production.
- Shipped: The order, or part of it, has shipped. Ship details include the
  ship date and tracking numbers/carrier links.

## Payment

- Dell places an authorization hold on the card equal to the order amount.
  The hold may remain for up to 5 business days; Dell charges the card when
  the order ships.
- If the hold disappears before shipment, that does not mean the order is
  cancelled.
- Card declines are often due to the issuer flagging unexpected activity,
  per-day purchase limits, or failed address/CVV verification. Dell cannot
  see the exact decline reason for privacy/security.
- To change payment method: cancel and re-enter the order before it enters
  manufacturing/fulfilment; this may impact delivery timelines and may
  forfeit expired promotions.
- Dell charges sales tax as required by law, varying by state. Exemption
  certificates can be submitted; contact Tax_Department@Dell.com for
  queries.
- California customers may see an Environmental (ECO) Fee on certain
  electronics — part of the Electronic Waste Recycling Act of 2003.
- Dell offers financing through Dell Pay (consumer private-label credit with
  Comenity Capital Bank) and through Dell Financial Services for businesses.

## Changing the order

- Changes are only possible before the order enters manufacturing/
  fulfilment. After that, the order must be cancelled and re-entered.
- To change billing or shipping address: cancel and re-order. Re-entering
  may change delivery timelines and forfeit promotions no longer valid.
- To cancel: sign in to My Account, select "Orders", then "Get Help" for
  eligibility. Many Dell-branded products cannot be cancelled once they
  enter production.

## Shipping and delivery

- Multiple order numbers: Depending on production times or availability,
  Dell may split an order into multiple orders with different estimated
  delivery dates. Each has its own tracking.
- Free standard delivery is available to everyone on everything, with no
  minimum purchase. Dell Rewards members get free expedited delivery on
  eligible products.
- Shipping methods: Express, Expedited, and Standard Ground. Exclusions
  apply for certain TVs, monitors, and batteries.
- The shipping method chosen at purchase is applied AFTER production/
  packaging — it does not include the time to build the system.
- Orders placed on or before 02:00 PM CST are eligible for expedited/
  express shipping.
- The Estimated Delivery Date in your confirmation email is an estimate;
  actual delivery may vary with carrier performance, weather, and holidays.
- APO/FPO/DPO addresses ship from the U.S. and can take up to 8 weeks;
  these shipments cannot be tracked during transit.
- Overseas-shipped orders: USA 7–10 business days, Canada 9–14 business
  days typical lead times.
- Multiple packages: When an order includes both a Dell system and
  accessories, they may be shipped separately.

## Receiving your order

- Inspect the package on delivery. Note any visible damage on the Proof of
  Delivery (POD) or delivery receipt before signing. Dell is not
  responsible for visible shipping damages not noted on the receipt.
- If you are not available, you can arrange for another adult to take
  delivery on your behalf.

## Tracking

Sign in to My Account, click Orders, select an order and "Track Order" to
see tracking numbers and carrier links. If a previously-shown tracking
number appears to show an older delivery date, carriers sometimes reuse
numbers; wait 24 hours for the information to update.

## Invoices and receipts

- Order confirmation email is sent immediately when the order is placed.
- Shipment confirmation is sent when the order ships.
- Invoices are typically sent on shipment. Tax adjustments to pre-payment
  tax estimates are reconciled on invoicing.

## Returns and refunds

- See the separate Dell US Return Policy document for the 30-day return
  window, restocking fees, and exclusions.
- Track returns via Dell.com/orders with the order number. Refunds may
  take 10–15 business days to appear on your original method of payment
  after Dell receives the return.

## Warranty questions

- See the Dell Limited Hardware Warranty document for details.
- For warranty issues, first run Dell SupportAssist or use dell.com/support
  with your Service Tag for troubleshooting.

## Contacting Dell

- Customer Service (U.S. consumers): 1-800-624-9897.
- Dell Financial Services (consumer Dell Pay accounts): visit
  dell.com/DellPayWelcome or Comenity Capital Bank.
