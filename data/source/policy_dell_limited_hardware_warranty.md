# Dell Limited Hardware Warranty (US and Canada)

Source: https://www.dell.com/en-us/lp/legal/art-limited-hardware-warranties
Company: Dell

Dell-branded hardware products purchased in the U.S. or Canada may come with
a 90-day, 1-year, 2-year, 3-year, 4-year, 5-year, or other limited hardware
warranty. Dell may offer different delivery methods for warranty service,
including parts and product dispatches, mail-in service, and onsite/in-home
service. Renewals and extensions may be available after purchase. To
determine the warranty that came with your product or the renewal/extension
you purchased, see your packing slip, invoice, receipt, or other sales
documentation.

## What is covered

This limited hardware warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship
in your Dell-branded hardware products, including Dell-branded peripheral
products.

## What is not covered

- Software, including the operating system and software added via Dell's
  factory-integration system, third-party software, or reloaded software.
- Non-Dell-branded products and accessories.
- Problems resulting directly or indirectly from:
  - External causes (accident, abuse, misuse, electrical power problems).
  - Servicing not authorized by Dell.
  - Usage not in accordance with product instructions.
  - Failure to follow instructions or perform preventive maintenance.
  - Using accessories, parts, or components not supplied by Dell.
  - For commercial hardware, using components Dell did not provide.
- Products with missing or altered service tags or serial numbers.
- Products for which Dell has not received payment.
- Normal wear and tear.

## How long it lasts

The warranty lasts for the time period indicated on your packing slip,
invoice, or receipt, with these exceptions:

- Laptop/notebook batteries: base 1-year limited hardware warranty
  regardless of system warranty length. Some products allow purchase of a
  battery with a 3-year limited warranty.
- Projector lamps: 1 year.
- Dell-certified and Dell-branded memory purchased separately from a
  system: lifetime limited hardware warranty.
- Standalone monitors: as listed on sales documentation. Monitors purchased
  with a system are covered by the system's warranty.
- External hard drives purchased simultaneously with a laptop/desktop/
  tablet/thin client: the longer of 2 years or the system's warranty.
- Earphones and remote in-line controls: 1 year.
- Printer-related: see printer consumables warranty below.
- Enterprise SSD/SATA/SAS/NVMe devices: typically 3 years max without
  ProSupport, 5 years max for PCIe SSD devices (limits on Device Life
  apply — defects covered, but not wearing to maximum write endurance).
- Networking products: see dell.com/networkingwarranty.

Unless required otherwise by law, the warranty starts on the packing-slip
invoice/receipt date for direct-from-Dell purchases, or on the date of the
original sales receipt for third-party retailer/reseller purchases. The
warranty period is not extended if Dell repairs or replaces a product or
parts.

1. Install any updates and resolve issues identified by Dell SupportAssist

dell.com/support or by phone. Have your Dell Service Tag or order number
ready. If purchased through a retailer, you may be required to provide

## What Dell does after contact

Dell first performs a remote diagnosis session. If Dell determines the
issue is a covered materials/workmanship defect and cannot be resolved
remotely, Dell may at its sole discretion:

- Dispatch a replacement part;
- Arrange for you to send your product or part back to Dell's repair depot;
  or
- Replace the part or product with a comparable part or product (new or
  refurbished).

If onsite/in-home service is included in your warranty, Dell may dispatch a
service technician to your U.S. or Canadian location. Both performance of
service and response times depend on time of day, service alternative
purchased, parts availability, geographical restrictions, weather
conditions, and the terms of the warranty.

Refurbished parts and products are inspected and tested for quality.
Replacement parts and products are covered for the remaining period of the
limited hardware warranty for the original product. Dell owns all parts
removed from repaired products; you are typically required to return
defective parts.

## Mail-in service variants

- Customer supplies box, pays shipping: Dell issues an RMA; customer ships
  product in original/equivalent packaging, prepays shipping, and insures
  the shipment. Dell pays return shipping to U.S. or Canadian addresses;
  otherwise freight collect.
- Customer supplies box, Dell pays shipping: Dell provides shipping
  instructions; customer packages product, arranges pickup, and follows
  Dell's carrier instructions. Dell pays standard shipping both ways for
  U.S. and Canadian addresses.
- Dell supplies box and pays shipping: Dell sends packaging, instructions,
  and a prepaid waybill; customer packages product and arranges pickup.
  Dell pays standard shipping both ways for U.S. and Canadian addresses.

If Dell ships a replacement product or part before receiving the defective
one, a valid credit card may be required. You must return the original
within 10 days of receiving the replacement. Failure to return may result
in a charge for the then-current standard price, or suspension of warranty
support until paid.

## Onsite/in-home service requirements

- An adult must be present at all times during the technician's visit.
- The technician must have full access to the system, working space,
  electricity, and a local telephone line.
- Dell is not obligated to provide service if the environment is unsafe
  (threats, pests, biohazards, hazardous chemicals, or insistence on
  varying locations).
- If no one is present when the technician arrives, a follow-up call may
  be charged.

## Transferring the warranty

Limited hardware warranties on systems may be transferred if the current
owner transfers ownership and records the transfer with Dell. Dell-certified
memory warranties may not be transferred. All transfers are at Dell's sole
discretion and subject to the original warranty terms and Dell's terms of
sale.

## Before returning / replacing

Back up data on hard drives and any storage device in the product. Remove
any confidential, proprietary, or personal information, and remove
removable media such as SIM/storage cards, DVDs/CDs, or PC cards. Dell is
not responsible for your data or removable media. Include only the
components Dell requests.

## Dell printer consumables warranty (U.S. and Canada)

- Dell ink cartridges: 2 years from invoice date.
- Dell toner cartridges: life of the cartridge under normal use and storage
  (original toner only, not refilled/remanufactured).
- Dell Premium Photo Paper and photo print packs: 90 days from invoice.

Call 1-800-822-8965 (U.S.) or 1-800-387-5757 (Canada) for return
authorization. This consumables warranty does not apply to damage from
misuse, abuse, accident, refilling, neglect, mishandling, or incorrect
environments.

## Limited Lifetime Warranty for Dell-branded tape media

Dell-branded tape media products are warranted against defects in material
and workmanship for the lifetime of the product if properly used and
maintained. If defective, Dell at its option will (a) repair, (b) replace,
or (c) refund, provided the product is returned with proof of purchase.
Coverage ends if you sell or transfer the product to another party. Does
not apply to failures from misuse, abuse, accident, neglect, mishandling,
improperly adjusted/maintained drives, incorrect environments, or wear from
ordinary use.
