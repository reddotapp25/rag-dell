## Overview

Dell's thinnest and lightest XPS laptop, powered by Qualcomm Snapdragon X Elite
(ARM). Marketed around all-day battery life and ultimate mobility. All
configurations ship with Windows 11 Home as a Copilot+ PC.

Starting price (as observed): $1,149.99 (with $300 limited-time savings off an
estimated value of $1,449.99). Build-to-order available starting at $999.99.

## Compatibility note

Because this model runs on ARM (Snapdragon), Dell recommends checking
Snapdragon compatibility with Windows 11 apps before purchase. Some Windows
software written for x86 may not run natively.

## Support

Base configuration includes 1-year Premium Support with Hardware and Software
(1–2 business day onsite after remote diagnosis). Accidental Damage Service is
optional.

## Customer sentiment (summary)

Customers praise the lightweight design, vivid display, and long-lasting
battery that often exceeds a full day. Some criticize the ARM-based Snapdragon
processor for incompatibility with common software, and many note the flat
keyboard and borderless touchpad as difficult to type or click accurately.
