# Dell US Return Policy

Source: https://www.dell.com/en-us/lp/dt/return-policy
Company: Dell

## Four-step return process

1. If you have a technical issue, first visit Dell's support page to resolve
   the issue or get a replacement device (dell.com/support with your Service
   Tag).
2. For other return reasons, go to dell.com/orders, enter your order number,
   select "Return/Replace Items", verify your identity, and follow the
   directions to submit your return.
3. Print the return label and package the device in its original packaging
   for pickup, or bring it to a local drop-off location.
4. Track your return online at Dell.com/orders with your order number. Dell
   will issue a credit or refund after they receive and validate the
   authorized return.

## Return window

- For all eligible purchases, Dell's return policy is 30 days from the date
  of your invoice, unless you have a separate agreement with Dell.
- You must initiate the return within this 30-day window.
- If approved, Dell issues a Credit Return Authorization (CRA) number.
- Products must be shipped back to Dell within 30 days of CRA issuance, and
  Dell must receive them within 30 calendar days of authorization; otherwise
  refund or credit may be reduced or eliminated.
- Dell sometimes offers promotions that extend the return period for a
  specific product; your return rights may differ in those cases.

## Software exclusions

You cannot return software at any time, unless it is:

- An operating system or application Dell installed on a returnable product
  that you are returning within that product's return period.
- Software on physical media (DVD/CD) that has not been opened or removed
  from its sealed package.
- Software delivered electronically for which you have not accepted the
  license agreement or other applicable terms.

Volume licenses are not returnable unless otherwise agreed in writing.

## Restocking fees

For approved returns initiated within the specified period, Dell will provide
a return shipping label at no cost and will not charge a restocking fee if:

- The product is defective.
- You have to return a product because Dell made an error.
- You are a consumer who purchased for personal use.

For all other permitted returns, Dell may charge a restocking fee of 15% of
the actual price you paid for the product (not including shipping/handling
or sales tax). Restocking fees are typically deducted from the refund or
credit.

## Promotions and bundles

Refunds for products bought in a bundle or promotion can be reduced:

- "Buy a computer and get a free printer." If you return the computer but
  keep the printer, Dell may reduce your refund by the value of the printer.
- "Buy a monitor and get a gift card." If you return the monitor but already
  spent the gift card, Dell may reduce your refund by the value of the gift
  card.

Dell Rewards points are also handled on return:

- If Dell Rewards were used as payment, those Rewards are credited back to
  your Rewards account for the returned portion.
- If Dell Rewards were earned on the purchase, Dell deducts the rewards
  amount awarded for the returned product from your balance.

## Preparing the product

After receiving a CRA number:

- All products must be in as-new condition.
- Back up any data on the product, then delete it. Remove all confidential,
  proprietary, and personal information.
- Remove any media (flash drives, CDs, PC cards) and any cables or
  port devices that did not come with the product.
- Return all products with all media, documentation, and items Dell
  included in your original shipment, in their original packaging.
- Commercial customers must ship the products at their own expense. If you
  do not insure the shipment, you accept the risk of loss or damage during
  shipment.

For partial returns, your refund or credit may be less than the invoice or
individual component price due to bundled or promotional pricing.

## Operating-system note

Systems configured with Intel 8th-generation or later CPUs are designed to
run optimally with Windows 11. Removing the factory-installed OS to run a
non-Windows 11 OS (such as Windows XP or Windows 10) may make the product
ineligible for return for a refund, and can cause system instability and
performance issues not covered by warranty, support, or service agreements.

## Refund timeline

- After Dell receives your returned product, it can take up to 10–15
  business days for the refund to be credited back to the original payment
  method.
- Track progress using the Return Status option in the Action Menu on your
  order.
- For approved returns, Dell refunds the actual price paid. The refund may
  be reduced by shipping-and-handling fees, any applicable restocking fees,
  or both.

## Denials

Dell reserves the right to deny returns in circumstances involving repeated
returns, violations of the Dell Terms of Sale, or other impermissible
activity.
