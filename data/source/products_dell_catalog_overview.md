# Dell Product Catalog Overview (US)

Source: compiled from Dell.com laptop and desktop category pages
Company: Dell

Dell sells the following main product categories directly to US consumers:

## Laptops

- XPS series: premium thin-and-light laptops. Current flagships are the
  XPS 13 (models 9350 Intel and 9345 Snapdragon). The XPS line emphasizes
  CNC-machined aluminum, InfinityEdge displays, and Copilot+ PC status on
  Windows configurations.
- Inspiron series: mainstream laptops aimed at everyday home use and
  education. Includes 14- and 15-inch models.
- Latitude series: business-class laptops with security features,
  manageability, and long-life support.
- Precision series: mobile workstations for CAD, engineering, and
  content-creation workloads with NVIDIA RTX professional graphics.
- Vostro series: small-business laptops balancing cost and features.
- Alienware and Dell G series: gaming laptops with NVIDIA GeForce RTX
  discrete GPUs.
- Dell Pro, Pro Plus, Pro Max, Pro Premium, Pro Rugged: newer naming
  umbrella for professional laptops and mobile workstations.

## Desktops and all-in-ones

- OptiPlex: business desktops (small, micro, tower, all-in-one).
- XPS desktop: consumer performance tower.
- Inspiron desktop and Inspiron all-in-one: mainstream consumer desktops.
- Alienware Aurora: gaming desktops.
- Precision workstations: desktop workstations.

## Accessories sold by Dell

- Monitors (Dell, UltraSharp, Alienware gaming displays).
- Docking stations and Thunderbolt docks.
- Wireless and wired keyboards, mice, and headsets.
- AC adapters, USB-C power adapters, and replacement batteries.
- Printers (limited mobile printer line).

## Pricing and inventory

- Free standard shipping on everything, no minimum purchase.
- Dell runs rotating promotions (Limited-Time Offers) that typically reduce
  the "Estimated Value" sticker by $200–$400. Examples observed at time of
  capture: $350 off XPS 13 9350 ($1,299.99 → $949.99), $300 off XPS 13
  9345 ($1,449.99 → $1,149.99).
- Customers can also configure a "Build Your Own" variant on product pages.
- A price-match guarantee is advertised; call 1-877-275-3355 or use click-
  to-chat to request a price match.
- Dell offers 12 months special financing on purchases of $799+ through a
  Dell Pay Credit Account, and awards Dell Rewards points on many
  purchases.

## Product availability notes

- Pricing, configurations, and availability change frequently. Offers for
  US Dell Systems Co. apply to new system online purchases only. Taxes and
  shipping charges are extra and vary.
- For legal and restocking details, see Dell's US Return Policy, Consumer
  Terms of Sale, and Limited Hardware Warranty.
